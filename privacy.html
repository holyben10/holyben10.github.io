Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Faceless Story Studio ("the app") is a desktop application that runs on the user's own Mac. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Google and YouTube data

If you choose to connect a YouTube channel, the app asks Google for these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos you create in the app to your channel, set their thumbnails and change their visibility, only when you confirm each upload.
  • youtube.readonly: to read your connected channel's name, handle, profile picture and subscriber count, so the app can show which channel is connected.

The app does not read, change or delete any other videos, comments, playlists or account information.

Where your data is stored

  • Your Google sign-in (OAuth refresh token) is stored only in the macOS Keychain on your own computer.
  • Your projects, scripts and videos are stored only on your own computer.
  • The app has no server. The developer does not receive, collect, store or sell any of your data.

Sharing

Data leaves your computer only to perform actions you request: videos you choose to upload are sent directly to YouTube (Google). Story text you ask the app to process may be sent to the AI services you configure (for example Anthropic Claude for writing, and ElevenLabs for voice-over), under their own privacy policies. Google user data is never shared with these services or any other third party, and is never used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Click Disconnect next to your channel in the app. This deletes the stored sign-in from your Keychain and revokes the app's access.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or through the Google security settings page.
  • Deleting the app and its data folder removes all data it stored on your computer.
