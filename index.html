Faceless Story Studio

Faceless Story Studio is a private desktop app for macOS that helps its owner write, narrate and animate storytelling videos (true crime, history and similar documentaries) and publish them to their own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Researches stories from public records and writes scripts with AI assistance.
  • Creates hand-drawn stickman animation, voice-over, captions and background music.
  • Optionally uploads finished videos to the user's own YouTube channel, only after the user reviews and confirms each upload.

How it runs

The app runs entirely on the user's own Mac. There is no Faceless Story Studio server and no user accounts: everything the app creates and every sign-in stays on the user's computer.

It is a private tool used by its developer and invited users, and is not offered to the public.
